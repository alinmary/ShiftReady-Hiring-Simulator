name: Pressure Mode scoring scope
description: Preservation and comparability rules for the added Round 2 assessment.
---

Pressure Mode is a Round 2 option, not a replacement for the existing first round or standard follow-up calls. Its Prioritisation, Delegation, and Closing the loop scores are additional, with a separate pressure average; the original seven-criterion average stays comparable across rounds.

**Why:** The user requested the attached multi-problem simulation as Round 2 and explicitly said not to change what was already included. Mixing new criteria into the original average would change the meaning of existing comparisons.

**How to apply:** Preserve historical reports and the original assessment. Add pressure-specific evidence and review controls only to Pressure Mode. Adaptive spacing is based on response gaps, not accent or fluency.
